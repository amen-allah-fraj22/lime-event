# Artist onboarding — what to send the pilot cohort

Ready-to-send instructions for the first artists invited to LIME EVENTS. Written to match exactly what the live sign-up wizard asks for (verified against the actual 4-step flow in code, not assumed) — so nothing here promises a step that doesn't exist yet, and nothing the wizard asks for is left unmentioned.

---

## Message to send artists (copy-paste ready)

> **You're invited to LIME EVENTS 🍋**
>
> LIME EVENTS is a new platform connecting Tunisian musicians directly with organizers — weddings, corporate events, festivals — no more endless WhatsApp back-and-forth.
>
> **Before you sign up, have ready:**
> 1. **A profile photo** (square-ish works best) and a **cover photo** (wider — this is what shows up when organizers browse artists, so pick your best shot)
> 2. **A short bio** — 2-3 sentences about who you are and what you play
> 3. **A demo link** — a YouTube or SoundCloud link to a recording or video (optional, but profiles with one get noticed more)
> 4. **Your city**, your **genres**, and the **instruments/role** you play (solo or band)
> 5. Any **technical or hospitality requirements** you always need (e.g. "2 mics, a mixer, vegan meals") — organizers see this before they reach out
>
> **About pricing**: you don't set a fixed rate on your profile. Organizers reach out first with what they need, and you quote and accept (or decline) each booking individually — full control, every time.
>
> **Sign-up takes about 5 minutes**: [invite link]
>
> Questions? Reply to this message directly.

---

## Why each item is on that list (so you can adjust the message without re-reading the code)

- **Photos** — genuinely required for a complete profile; the cover photo specifically is what renders on the public browse grid, so a strong first impression there matters.
- **Bio, city, genres, instruments** — step 1 and 2 of the live wizard, collected before an artist can publish.
- **Demo link** — step 3, optional (`Demo Track / Video URL`, no `required` attribute in the form).
- **Requirements** — step 4, free text, and this is also where the wizard itself tells the artist "your fee isn't shown here — organizers reach out first." Worth repeating in the invite message so no one expects to set a price upfront.
- **Not mentioned**: a fixed price/rate field, because there isn't one in the current flow — pricing is negotiated per booking (see Requirements step copy above). Don't promise a pricing field in outreach copy; it would be wrong the moment someone opens the app.
